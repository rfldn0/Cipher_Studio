Our Mission

Founded by Victor R Tabuni, Cipher Studio is an independent game development studio specializing in therapeutic gaming experiences that promote mental health and personal growth.

We create open-world exploration games designed with evidence-based wellness principles. Our approach combines engaging gameplay mechanics with psychological research to develop experiences that provide stress relief and encourage self-discovery.

Through carefully crafted narratives and interactive environments, we build digital spaces where players can explore both virtual worlds and aspects of their own personal journey.
