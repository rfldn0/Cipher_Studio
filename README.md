## About

Cipher Studio is an independent game development studio founded by Victor R Tabuni, dedicated to creating therapeutic gaming experiences that promote mental health and personal growth. We develop evidence-based interactive experiences that serve as digital tools for wellness, stress relief, and emotional development.

Our studio focuses on open-world exploration games designed with psychological research principles, combining engaging gameplay mechanics with therapeutic methodologies to create meaningful digital experiences.

## Philosophy

**Mental Wellness Integration**: Every project incorporates evidence-based therapeutic principles designed by mental health professionals.

**Creative Expression**: Our games provide safe digital environments where players can explore personal identity and creative potential.

**Meaningful Exploration**: Open-world designs that encourage curiosity, discovery, and personal reflection through interactive narratives.

**Character Development**: Progression systems that mirror real-world personal growth and skill development.

**Accessible Therapeutic Tools**: Making mental health resources available through engaging, non-clinical gaming experiences.

## Technical Framework

**Game Development**: Unity 3D, Unreal Engine 5, custom therapeutic mechanics framework

**Programming Languages**: C#, JavaScript, Python for data analysis and user behavior tracking

**Design Methodology**: Evidence-based therapeutic design, accessibility-first UI/UX principles

**Web Technologies**: HTML5, CSS3, responsive design for companion applications

**Virtual Reality**: Oculus SDK, OpenXR, SteamVR for immersive therapeutic experiences

**Research Integration**: Collaboration with licensed mental health professionals for validation

## Core Therapeutic Elements

**Open World Design**: Vast explorable environments promoting player autonomy and freedom of choice in healing journey.

**Creative Expression**: Comprehensive creative tools allowing unlimited artistic expression and identity exploration.

**Character Growth**: Meaningful progression systems reflecting real-world personal development milestones.

**Mystery Elements**: Engaging narrative puzzles promoting cognitive stimulation and problem-solving skills.

**Mental Wellness**: Direct integration of evidence-based therapeutic techniques within gameplay mechanics.

**Therapeutic Design**: Safe, supportive virtual environments designed for emotional healing and growth.
